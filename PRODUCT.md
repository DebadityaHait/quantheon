# Product

<!-- impeccable:product-schema 1 -->

## Platform

web

## Users

Derivatives traders and risk managers using the desk throughout the working day.

## Product Purpose

Quantheon is an agentic derivatives pricing and hedging desk: users price and book products, assess risk, plan hedges and manage desk operations in one workspace.

## Positioning

An agent coordinates workflows and deterministic quantitative tools supply pricing and risk calculations. Human review remains important for consequential actions.

## Operating Context

Users move between the agent desk, pricing, positions, portfolio risk, scenarios, hedging and operational workflows. Work involves dense financial data, potentially long-running tasks and approval decisions.

## Capabilities and Constraints

Preserve existing workflows, functionality, light and dark themes, and comfortable and compact density modes. The application is a single-operator development application, not a multi-tenant hosted service.

## Brand Commitments

Quantheon — Agentic derivatives pricing and hedging desk. The prior visual identity and navigation may be replaced.

## Evidence on Hand

Current dark-mode captures live in `docs/screenshots/quantheon-*.png`. They use a synthetic portfolio; the displayed valuations, Greeks and backtest series were seeded for illustration, not calculated by the pricing engine.

## Product Principles

- Make financial information legible and comparable.
- Preserve traceability and human control over consequential actions.
- Support frequent work without sacrificing clarity for decoration.
