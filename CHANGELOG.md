# Changelog

Notable changes to Quantheon are documented here in the [Keep a Changelog](https://keepachangelog.com/en/1.1.0/) format.

## [Unreleased]

### Added

- Quantheon workspace, including pricing and booking, portfolio risk, hedging, scenarios, confirmations, settlement, reports and the agent desk.
- Dark-mode product screenshots and local setup guidance.
- A bundled deterministic pricing engine, including its third-party license and notice.

### Changed

- Refresh the workspace navigation, shared design tokens and responsive page selector.
- Consolidate engine naming while preserving compatibility with existing data and integrations.
- Remove obsolete planning documents that are not referenced by the application or its operational guides.
